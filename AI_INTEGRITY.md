# AI Integrity Layer

AI is a silent second observer, not a final judge.

Allowed responsibilities:
- audio quality diagnostics;
- constrained position alignment against the expected approved Quran text;
- possible omission/insertion/substitution/repetition/hesitation observations;
- review prioritization;
- panel variance assistance.

Forbidden responsibility:
- automatic score deduction based solely on AI output.

Every production AI capability is versioned and has a certification state: Certified, Beta or Unsupported. Certification must be per capability and riwaya, backed by a gold dataset and measured false-positive/false-negative performance. If the AI service fails, judging continues.
