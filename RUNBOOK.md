# Competition Day Runbook

## Open day
Verify edge, storage, microphones, committees, judges, approved Quran source, queue engine and backup power. Optional AI may be degraded without blocking the day.

## During day
Operate by exception. Track unresolved incidents, missing judge submissions, audio failures and queue bottlenecks. Do not manually edit sealed results.

## Emergency
Pause new calls, preserve active sessions, record incident, communicate instructions, then resume through a controlled action.

## Close day
Reconcile pending submissions, verify recordings according to policy, resolve sync conflicts, confirm backup status and create a missing-items report.
